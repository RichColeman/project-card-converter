PIP

A Corporate Roguelike Deckbuilder

You've been placed on a Performance Improvement Plan. So has everyone above you, eventually. Build a deck of workplace tactics, read your manager, and climb the ladder one PIP at a time — until the system finally gets you.

Each week you draw a hand and spend Focus playing cards. Friday, your manager and the office push back — their next move is telegraphed, so plan ahead.

▸ Survive a job (hit PIP by Week 4) or Escape it (hit Job Search — but only from Week 3).
▸ Mental Health carries between jobs. Your deck and Office Perks do too. Heat and deadlines reset.
▸ Get fired, burn out, or get caught and the run ends. Score = how far you climbed.
"In theory, it's a support mechanism. In practice, it's a paper trail."
